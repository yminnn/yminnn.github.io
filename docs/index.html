Brains. Signals. Silicon. I build the bridge between them.

Researcher at imec & University of Groningen, building low-power circuits and telemetry systems for next-generation brain-computer interfaces.

# ABOUT

My work began with energy-efficient frequency synthesis and wireless transceivers. I now apply that circuit and system expertise to brain-computer interfaces, where sensing, computation and telemetry must operate under extreme power and bandwidth constraints.

My research interests include:

  • Brain-Computer Interfaces
  • Wireless Communication
  • Phase-Locked Loops
  • Analog-Digital Converters
  • Neuromorphic Computing
